Step back into your Cardboard photos

Choose your .vr.jpg files, pick one, then tap Enter VR. You'll see it in 3D with the sound you recorded. Your photos stay on this device.

Choose photos to get started.

    In VR: pull the right trigger for the next photo, the left trigger for the previous one. Press the Meta button to leave VR.

    Outside VR: drag the picture to look around.

    Black above and below the scene is normal. Cardboard Camera doesn't capture the sky or ground.
